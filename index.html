5-Minute Energy Boost

Wake up your mind and muscles between study sessions.

  1. March in place - 30 seconds
  2. Arm circles - 10 forward, 10 backward
  3. Bodyweight squats - 10 gentle repetitions
  4. Deep breathing stretch - 3 slow breaths

🤸

Desk & Neck Reset

Relieve neck, back, and shoulder stiffness from laptop use.

  1. Neck rolls - 5 slow rotations each side
  2. Shoulder shrugs - Lift shoulders & hold 3 sec
  3. Seated spine twist - Gentle turn left and right
  4. Wrist flexor stretch - Extend arms and flex hands
15 Minutes Cardio
🚶

Brisk Campus Walk

Clear mental fatigue and get healthy natural sunshine.

  1. Warm-up pace - 3 minutes easy strolling
  2. Brisk walking - 8 minutes fast walking pace
  3. Calf raises - 15 raises on flat ground
  4. Cool down - 3 minutes relaxed breathing
